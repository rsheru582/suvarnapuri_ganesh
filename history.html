The Journey of Suvarnapuri Friends Youth

Built purely on the strength of friendship and selfless service by all youth members of Suvarnapuri Friends Youth Soan.

How It All Began

Suvarnapuri Friends Youth in Soan was formed with one single goal: to unite the youth of our community through the sacred occasion of Ganesh Navaratri. With no individual leadership or hierarchy, all youth members come together as one family to organize the celebrations, manage the pandap, prepare prasadams, and serve the people.

2022 Milestone

Peacock Throne & Clay Idol

The 2022 utsav featured the iconic Mayureshwar idol seated gracefully on a peacock throne, surrounded by traditional clay Ganesh idols offered by devotees.

2023 Milestone

Royal Blue Floral Mandapam

The youth designed a majestic royal setup with blue and white floral canopies, daily Deepotsavam, and community prasadam distribution for all visitors.
